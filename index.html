How it works

Three taps between your bed and your lecture hall.

No surge pricing, no strangers, no 6:30 bus. Just the people already making your exact trip.

01

Verify your .edu

Sign up with your university email. Everyone on commutr is a real, verified student at your school. No one else gets in.

02

Find your route

Pick the day, the time block and your pickup point. See exactly who's driving that trip, what they drive, and what they charge.

03

Keep the seat

Book once and it repeats every week. Same driver, same 7:40, same $3, and a tap to cancel when class does.

Why commutr

Rideshare apps weren't built for a 10-minute commute you make 400 times a year.

Students only, verified

Every account is tied to a university email, and every trip ends with a rating. You're riding with classmates, not the internet.

Built for the repeat

commutr is designed around your schedule, not one-off trips. Post or book a recurring ride once and the whole semester is handled.

Pickups where you live

Rides start at real places: your complex, your lot, your corner. Never a pin you have to walk five minutes to find.

A price, not a fare

Drivers set a flat cost per seat to cover gas and parking. You see it before you book, and it doesn't move because it started raining.
